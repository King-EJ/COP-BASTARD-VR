COP BASTARD VR  -  VR mod for "COP BASTARD" (Unity 2021.1, Mono x64, built-in renderer, BepInEx 5)
==================================================================================================
Version 0.1.7 (test build)

INSTALL
  1. unzip to game folder, only tested with virtual desktop XR,
  2. Start your OpenXR runtime first (Virtual Desktop / SteamVR / Quest Link), then the game.
     HAVE TO USE KEYBOARD AT **"PRESS ANY KEY TO START"**

VIEW
  * Play: you are the cop's eyes; you walk where you look. The right stick turns you (snap 30 degrees, or smooth).
  * THE GUN IS IN YOUR RIGHT HAND: the game's arms + weapon follow the right controller and you shoot where it
    points. A laser shows where (the game's crosshair is hidden). Weapon place in the hand: [Weapon] WeaponPosition /
    WeaponRotation; HideArms = only the weapon; laser colour / thickness / length there too. VR menu: weapon in hand, aim with hand, laser.
  * The HUD floats in front of your eyes and turns with your head (VR menu "HUD in front of your eyes").
  * Menus / pause / comics: the game's picture with its menus on a screen in front of you.

CONTROLS (the VR controllers are an Xbox gamepad - the game's own gamepad controls and button pictures)
-
  Left stick ............ move
  
  Right stick ......... left / right = turn
  
  Right stick.......... up = D-pad RIGHT (next weapon)
  
 Right stick........... down = D-pad LEFT (previous weapon)
 
 X + Y together ........ START (pause menu)
 
 Menus: point with the right controller (laser) and pull the right trigger to click; or left / right stick =
         move, A = choose, B = back.
         
 Both stick clicks: tap = re-centre the view
 
 Both stick Hold 1.2 s = VR settings menu
                     (stick up / down choose, left / right change, A do, B close).
                     
 CHEATS (VR menu "Cheats", or [Cheats] in the config; only for you, enemies are normal)
  * Unlimited health (on): you take no damage.
  * Unlimited ammo (on): the clip refills after every shot - no reloading.
